The pipeline that builds your world

Six agents working in sequence, each with a single job.

Persistent Story Memory

Every chapter feeds back into the graph. Names, places, and plot threads stay consistent across your entire storyworld.

Writer Agent

Generates a full draft from your project metadata, chapter direction, and prior story summaries.

Continuity Check

Cross-references the draft against your established lore to catch contradictions before they ship.

Revision Loop

Scores the draft against quality thresholds and triggers rewrites until the bar is met.

Summarizer

Produces structured chapter summaries and plot memory for future context and recall.
